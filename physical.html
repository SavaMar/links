Questions

Before you book.

Yes. I work with any physical business where the space and the experience are better than what shows up online — coworking spaces, restaurants, clinics, studios, retail. If people visit your location, I can help you look like what you actually are.

Do I need to be on camera?

No. Most of the content I create focuses on the space, the atmosphere, and natural moments. If you want to be on camera, great — but it's not required.

English, German, and French. Content and captions can be in any language. Your team doesn't need to speak English.
